# Sources

Original source PDFs and their converted text versions. The bibliography (what
each source is and why it matters) lives in
[`../references/README.md`](../references/README.md); this folder holds the
artifacts themselves.

## Convention

- PDFs are gitignored globally (`*.pdf`) EXCEPT those placed here
  (`!sources/*.pdf` in `.gitignore`), so public-domain or owner-licensed source
  PDFs may be tracked here while copyrighted textbooks stay out of git.
- Converted text versions (for searching and reading-note generation) are named
  to match their PDF and may be tracked.

## Public-domain primary sources worth holding here

- Cook's official Clay Millennium Prize problem statement (freely distributed by
  the Clay Mathematics Institute).
- Preprint (arXiv) versions of the barrier papers and the Williams lower bound,
  where the authors have posted them.
